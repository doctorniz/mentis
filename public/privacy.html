Mentis privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Mentis keeps your notes, PDFs, drawings, tasks and everything else you write as ordinary files in a folder you choose. We run no server that holds your data.

What stays on your device

Your files, the search index built from them, your settings and your API keys never leave your device unless you switch on one of the features below. We do not receive them and cannot read them.

What leaves your device, and only when you use it

  • Cloud sync (Dropbox). If you connect Dropbox, Mentis sends your vault's files to and from your own Dropbox, using the access you grant. Dropbox's privacy policy applies to what it holds. We never see your files or your Dropbox sign-in.
  • AI chat. Chat is off until you turn it on in Settings › AI. When you ask a question, Mentis sends it, together with excerpts from your notes that it found relevant, to the provider you chose (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini), using your own API key, or to an Ollama or llama.cpp server you run. That provider's terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. Mentis does not send chat anywhere else.
  • Bookmark previews. When you save a bookmark, Mentis asks the Microlink service (api.microlink.io) for the page's title and image, which sends it the address you saved, and loads the site's icon from Google's favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons), which receives the site's domain name.
  • Voice notes. Recording and transcription happen on your device. The first time you transcribe, Mentis downloads a speech model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which sees that download request and your network address but none of your audio.
  • Links and images in your notes. A web link or remote image in a note is fetched from the site it points to when you open it, as in any web browser.

Permissions

  • Microphone for voice notes, and camera for photos you take into a note. Neither is used until you press the control for it.
  • Files and folders you pick, so Mentis can read and write your vault there.
  • Local network, only if you point chat at an Ollama or llama.cpp server on your own network.

This website

mentis.ink is hosted by Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run the service. The web version of the app stores your files in your browser, or in a folder you pick, on your device. It does not upload them.

Children

Mentis collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical use

Mentis is not designed for clinical or patient data. Please do not store it there.

Changes
